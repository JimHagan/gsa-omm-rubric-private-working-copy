## Scoring Summary

| Level | Summary |
|---|---|
| 1 | No inventory. Service count unknown. |
| 2 | Rough count; not authoritative. Ownership gaps common. |
| 3 | Formal inventory maintained; owners assigned for critical services. |
| 4 | Inventory in service catalog; automatically updated from deployments. |
| 5 | Self-healing inventory; new services auto-register on provision. |
