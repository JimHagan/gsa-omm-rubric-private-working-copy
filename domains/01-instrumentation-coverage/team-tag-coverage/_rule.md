## Scoring Summary

| Level | Summary |
|---|---|
| 1 | No required tag taxonomy. Tags applied ad hoc or not at all. Team tag coverage below 30%. |
| 2 | Tag taxonomy documented (team, environment, criticality required). Compliance voluntary. Coverage 30–69%. |
| 3 | Tag policy enforced. Active remediation program for non-compliant services. Criticality tag in use for alert routing. Coverage 70–89%. |
| 4 | Enforced via CI gate or NR Governance. All environments consistently tagged — enables reliable NR Scorecard rule scoping. Coverage 90–98%. |
| 5 | Tags auto-synced from CMDB or service catalog. New entities inherit required taxonomy on provision. Coverage 99%+. |
