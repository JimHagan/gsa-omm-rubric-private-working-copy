## Scoring Summary

| Level | Summary |
|---|---|
| 1 | No required tag taxonomy. Tags applied ad hoc or not at all. |
| 2 | Tag taxonomy documented (team, environment, criticality required). Compliance voluntary. |
| 3 | Tag policy enforced. Active remediation program reducing gap. Criticality tag in use for alert routing. |
| 4 | Enforced via CI gate or NR Governance. Enables reliable NR Scorecard rule scoping by environment. |
| 5 | Tags auto-synced from CMDB or service catalog. New entities inherit required taxonomy on provision. |
