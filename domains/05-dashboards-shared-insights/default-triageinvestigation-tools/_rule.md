## Scoring Summary

| Level | Summary |
|---|---|
| 1 | No consistent starting point. Engineers improvise per incident — different people open different tools. No agreed operational view exists. |
| 2 | Two or three tools in common use during triage. NR is one of them but not consistently the first. Engineers context-switch to get a complete picture. |
| 3 | NR is the default and first tool opened when an alert fires. Other tools may still be used for specialist tasks but NR provides the agreed starting point. |
| 4 | NR is the single triage interface. Other tools have been deprecated, integrated into NR, or are explicitly scheduled for retirement. Engineers do not need to leave NR to complete an investigation. |
| 5 | NR AI surfaces answers within the triage interface without engineers constructing queries. Context-switching to any other tool has not occurred in production incident workflows for six months or more. |
