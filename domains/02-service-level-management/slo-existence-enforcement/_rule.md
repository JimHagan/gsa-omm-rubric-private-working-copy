## Scoring Summary

| Level | Summary |
|---|---|
| 1 | No SLOs defined. Reliability = uptime check or informal agreement. |
| 2 | SLOs exist in dashboards or docs; not enforced in decisions. |
| 3 | SLOs defined in NR for critical services; reviewed in engineering ops. |
| 4 | SLOs integrated into change and release workflows; error budgets actively referenced. |
| 5 | Predictive SLO management; auto-calibrating targets; SLO-governed release pipeline. |
