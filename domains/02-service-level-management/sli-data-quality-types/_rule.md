## Scoring Summary

| Level | Summary |
|---|---|
| 1 | No SLIs. Availability approximated by uptime ping. |
| 2 | SLIs exist but based on arbitrary thresholds, not observed baselines. |
| 3 | SLIs from observed P95 baselines and REQUIRED SLAs where relevant; updated on engineering cadence. |
| 4 | Multi-layer SLIs: user/customer, service/platform, and business outcome layers. |
| 5 | SLIs auto-calibrate based on deployment impact analysis. |
