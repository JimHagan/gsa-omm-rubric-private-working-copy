# References

## Accelerate: The Science of Lean Software and DevOps
**URL:** https://itrevolution.com/product/accelerate/
**Summary:** Foundational research establishing the four DORA metrics — deployment frequency, lead time for changes, change failure rate, and mean time to recover — as the key predictors of software delivery performance. Based on six years of survey data, provides the empirical basis for distinguishing elite performers from low performers and underpins L3–L5 scoring criteria for delivery velocity and recovery time.

## Accelerate State of DevOps Report 2024
**URL:** https://dora.dev/research/2024/dora-report/
**Summary:** The 2024 annual DORA research report covering software delivery performance benchmarks. Introduces a five-metric model adding rework rate as a measure of quality debt. Provides current elite/high/medium/low performer thresholds used as L3–L5 scoring calibration anchors.

## Accelerate State of AI-Assisted Software Development Report 2025
**URL:** https://dora.dev/research/2025/
**Summary:** DORA's 2025 research on the impact of AI-assisted development on software delivery performance. Introduces a seven-team-archetype model and examines conditions under which AI adoption improves vs. degrades delivery stability. Findings on platform engineering capability maturity inform L3–L5 scoring calibration for platform health and developer experience domains.

## A History of DORA's Software Delivery Metrics
**URL:** https://dora.dev/insights/dora-metrics-history/
**Summary:** Authoritative account of how DORA's software delivery metrics have evolved since 2014, including the replacement of MTTR with Failed Deployment Recovery Time in the five-metric model. Confirms the current canonical set of metrics and their measurement methodology.

## Engineering Excellence: Maturity Framework Introduction
**URL:** https://docs.newrelic.com/docs/new-relic-solutions/observability-maturity/engineering-excellence/introduction/
**Summary:** New Relic's Engineering Excellence value driver framework. Covers operational efficiency, proactive change management, and enhanced security posture. Provides NR-specific framing for Developer Experience and Platform Engineering domain rubric design and PE Track acceleration path activities.
