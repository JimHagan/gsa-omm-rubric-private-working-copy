# References

## Platform Engineering Maturity Model
**URL:** https://tag-app-delivery.cncf.io/whitepapers/platform-eng-maturity-model/
**Summary:** CNCF's authoritative maturity model for internal developer platforms. Defines five maturity levels (Provisional → Operational → Scalable → Optimizing → Objective) and articulates the golden path concept — a paved road of curated tooling choices that reduces cognitive load for service teams. Primary authority for platform-as-a-product criteria at L4 and golden path adoption criteria.

## Platforms Definition White Paper
**URL:** https://tag-app-delivery.cncf.io/whitepapers/platforms/
**Summary:** CNCF's foundational definition of internal developer platforms, distinguishing them from infrastructure teams and shared services. Articulates the platform-as-a-product principle requiring platforms to be designed around developer needs with a product owner and roadmap.

## State of Platform Engineering Report, Volume 4
**URL:** https://platformengineering.org/blog/platform-engineering-maturity-in-2026
**Summary:** Current industry data on platform engineering adoption and maturity. Key findings: 45.5% of dedicated platform teams remain reactive despite formal budgets; 29.6% of organizations lack measurement practices for platform health. Provides benchmark data for contextualizing L1–L3 scoring.
