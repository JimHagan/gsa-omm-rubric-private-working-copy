## Scoring Summary

| Level | Summary |
|---|---|
| 1 | Three or more disconnected observability tools in active use with no integration between them. Engineers context-switch between tools during every incident. No shared view of service health exists. |
| 2 | Non-NR tools inventoried and catalogued. Consolidation acknowledged as a problem but no active program started. Tools continue to operate in parallel. |
| 3 | Consolidation program underway. At least one major non-NR tool deprecated or replaced by NR capability in the past 12 months. A roadmap with target dates exists for remaining tools. |
| 4 | NR is the primary operational tool. Remaining non-NR tools either feed data into NR via OpenTelemetry or integration, or have a committed retirement date within 6 months. |
| 5 | NR is the single observability plane for operational workflows. All previous consolidation complete. No active non-NR tools in production incident triage or monitoring workflows. |
