## Scoring Summary

| Level | Summary |
|---|---|
| 1 | >60% of incidents classified as critical. No effective severity tiering — every alert pages on-call immediately. |
| 2 | 40–60% critical rate. Severity tiers defined in alert policies but inconsistently applied. Team acknowledges the over-alerting problem. |
| 3 | 25–39% critical rate. Severity tiers actively applied. "Critical" has an agreed team definition — direct user or revenue impact. Actively reducing via NR AQM review. |
| 4 | 15–24% critical rate. Agreed definition documented and enforced. Mean Time to Acknowledge (MTTA) tracked within a defined SLA. |
| 5 | <15% critical rate. Critical rate tracked as an engineering KPI with a defined target. Trending toward that target. |
