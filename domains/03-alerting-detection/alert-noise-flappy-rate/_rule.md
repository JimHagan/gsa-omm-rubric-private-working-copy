## Scoring Summary

| Level | Summary |
|---|---|
| 1 | >40% flappy rate. On-call conditioned to ignore alerts. Real incidents buried. NR AQM (Alert Quality Management) dashboard not deployed. |
| 2 | 20–40% flappy rate. Alert fatigue acknowledged. NR AQM deployed but results not acted upon. No active noise reduction program. |
| 3 | 10–19% flappy rate. NR AQM actively used to prioritise noise reduction. Flappy rate on a sustained downward trend over 90 days. |
| 4 | 4–9% flappy rate. Alert quality reviewed monthly using AQM data. Dynamic NRQL thresholds deployed on revenue-path services. Mean Time to Close tracked within SLA. |
| 5 | <4% flappy rate. Burn-rate SLO alerts are the dominant pattern on services with active SLOs. Noise is the exception. |
